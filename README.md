# Government of Canada Fixed Income Term Structure Analysis

Analyzed the 0–5Y Government of Canada term structure using Python.

## Methods
- Constructed yield curves from 10 Government of Canada bonds
- Bootstrapped discount factors and spot rates from coupon bond prices
- Derived 1-year forward rates
- Analyzed yield and forward-rate movements using covariance matrices and PCA

## Tools
Python, pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib

## Key Outputs
- Government of Canada YTM curves
- Bootstrapped spot curve
- Forward-rate curve
- PCA of yield and forward-rate changes
